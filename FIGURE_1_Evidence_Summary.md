# FIGURE 1: Evidence Summary of Major Trials in Stable Angina

## Comparative Evidence from Five Landmark Randomized Controlled Trials

| Trial | Year | N | Population | Intervention | Follow-up | Primary Endpoint | Prognostic Outcome (Death/MI) | Symptomatic Outcome | Key Finding |
|-------|------|---|------------|--------------|-----------|------------------|-------------------------------|---------------------|-------------|
| **COURAGE** | 2007 | 2,287 | Stable angina<br/>Angiographic stenosis | Angio-guided PCI + OMT<br/>vs.<br/>OMT alone | 4.6 years | Death or MI | **19.0% vs 18.5%**<br/>p=0.62<br/>*(No benefit)* | Modest early benefit | PCI + OMT did not reduce death/MI vs OMT alone |
| **ORBITA** | 2018 | 200 | Stable angina<br/>≥70% stenosis<br/>**ON antianginal meds** | PCI + OMT<br/>vs.<br/>**Sham + OMT** | 6 weeks | Exercise time | Not assessed | **No significant benefit**<br/>+16.6s, p=0.20 | PCI provided no symptomatic benefit over placebo when on OMT |
| **ORBITA-2** | 2023 | 301 | Stable angina<br/>≥70% stenosis<br/>**OFF antianginal meds** | PCI<br/>vs.<br/>**Sham procedure** | 12 weeks | Angina symptom score | Not assessed | **Significant benefit**<br/>OR 2.21, p<0.001<br/>3× ↑ angina freedom | PCI provided clear symptomatic benefit when OFF medications |
| **ISCHEMIA** | 2020 | 5,179 | Moderate-severe ischemia | Invasive (PCI/CABG) + OMT<br/>vs.<br/>Conservative OMT | 3.2 years | CV death, MI, hosp for UA | **13.3% vs 15.5%**<br/>HR 0.93 (0.80-1.08)<br/>*(No benefit)* | Early benefit<br/>Diminished by 3y | Invasive strategy did not reduce death/MI even with documented ischemia |

## Key Insights from the Evidence:

### Prognostic Benefit (Preventing Death/MI):
- ❌ **Angiography-guided PCI**: No benefit (COURAGE, ISCHEMIA)
- ❌ **Ischemia-guided PCI**: No benefit (ISCHEMIA)
- ❌ **FFR-guided PCI**: No mortality benefit; trend to ↓MI not significant (FAME-2)
- ✅ **Conclusion**: **Routine PCI does NOT reduce death or MI in stable angina**

### Symptomatic Benefit:
- ❌ **When ON antianginal medications**: No benefit vs placebo (ORBITA)
- ✅ **When OFF antianginal medications**: Clear benefit vs placebo (ORBITA-2)
- ✅ **FFR-guided approach**: Reduced urgent revascularization (FAME-2)
- ✅ **Conclusion**: **Symptomatic benefit is context-dependent**

### Patient Selection Matters:
- **FFR >0.80**: Excellent outcomes on OMT alone (FAME-2 registry)
- **Medication status**: Determines whether PCI provides symptomatic relief
- **High-risk anatomy**: Excluded from COURAGE/ISCHEMIA (left main, severe proximal LAD)

**Figure 1 Legend**: Comparison of five landmark randomized controlled trials evaluating PCI for stable angina. COURAGE and ISCHEMIA demonstrated no prognostic benefit from PCI. ORBITA showed no symptomatic benefit when patients were on antianginal medications, while ORBITA-2 showed clear benefit when medications were discontinued. FAME-2 showed reduction in urgent revascularization with FFR-guided PCI but no mortality benefit. OMT = optimal medical therapy; FFR = fractional flow reserve; CV = cardiovascular; MI = myocardial infarction; UA = unstable angina; revasc = revascularization; NS = not significant.
